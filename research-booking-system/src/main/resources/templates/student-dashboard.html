Status Lifecycle & Guidelines

PENDING
Awaiting Laboratory Approval

The booking request has been submitted by the student and is queued for supervisor confirmation. Students can cancel pending requests at any time before acceptance.

CONFIRMED
Confirmed Laboratory Appointment

The time slot has been accepted by the laboratory supervisor. Capacity has been allocated for the student. Cancellation is permitted in demo mode or subject to departmental notice policies.

CANCELLED
Session Cancelled

The booking was cancelled either by the student or by the laboratory researcher. The slot has been released back to general availability. Cancelled requests remain in the audit history for record-keeping and cannot be cancelled again.

Status Lifecycle & Guidelines

PENDING
Awaiting Laboratory Approval

The booking request has been submitted to the database and queued for faculty review. Students can cancel pending requests at any time before acceptance.

CONFIRMED
Confirmed Laboratory Appointment

The time slot and station have been confirmed by the laboratory supervisor. Capacity has been allocated. If you cannot attend, please cancel promptly to release the slot for other students.

CANCELLED
Session Cancelled

The reservation was cancelled either by the student or the supervisor. The slot capacity is released back to the laboratory schedule. Cancelled requests remain in the audit log.
